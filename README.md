# Hi, I’m Diego Osorio 👋

I’m a mechatronics engineer pursuing an MSc in **Systems, Control and Robotics at KTH Royal Institute of Technology**. I’m based in Stockholm, where I’m also an intern at **Furhat Robotics** in the prototyping and concepting team.

My background in mechatronics has given me experience working across software, electronics, and mechanical systems. Before moving to Sweden, I also worked as a laboratory assistant in Colombia, supporting practical learning and laboratory activities.

I enjoy working on projects that bring these areas together to create systems that interact with the physical world. My recent work has focused on **robot perception, mapping, localization, and navigation**, from helping a mobile manipulator localize with LiDAR to exploring image-based navigation and 3D scene understanding.

## Projects

### 🤖 Autonomous Mobile Manipulation

Our team built a ROS 2 robot that explores an indoor environment, identifies objects, and picks them up. I led the mapping and localization work, using wheel odometry, IMU data, and LiDAR scan matching to estimate and correct the robot’s pose.

[View the project repository](https://github.com/clefal/dd2419_ws)

### 🧭 Mapless Robotics

We explored in simulation how a robot can follow an image-space reference trajectory for collaborative exploration and object search and retrieval. The repository includes a video demonstration and instructions for running the system.

[Watch the demo and explore the code](https://github.com/DefineDan/mapless-robotics#readme)

### 🎯 KTH Edge AI & Robotics Challenge

I’m working on integrating multi-robot exploration, target detection and rescue in a ROS 2 and Gazebo arena.

[Implementation](https://github.com/diegoosoriof/edgeai_robot_ws) · [Competition webpage](https://dongjw-hit.github.io/kth-edgeai-robotics-challenge/index.html)

### 🦾 Robot Manipulation and Industrial Robotics

As part of my robotics coursework in Colombia, I worked on robot kinematics, manipulation, trajectory planning, and industrial robot programming.

- **PhantomX manipulation** — Implemented inverse kinematics and developed pick-and-place and task-space control using ROS, MATLAB, and Python. [View Lab 3](https://github.com/dosoriof/lab3_rob_unal)
- **ABB IRB 140 trajectory planning** — Designed a 3D-printed marker holder, calibrated the tool, and programmed drawing trajectories in RobotStudio and RAPID. [View Lab 4](https://github.com/dosoriof/lab4_rob_unal/blob/main/README.md)
- **Digital input control** — Used digital inputs and work objects to select between drawing trajectories on horizontal and inclined surfaces. [View Lab 5](https://github.com/dosoriof/lab5_rob_unal/blob/main/README.md)
